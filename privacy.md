# ReliefLedger privacy

Updated October 6, 2026.

ReliefLedger is a product of Sire Run, Inc. This website describes ReliefLedger's overtime management software. It is not a portal for facility, employee, or inmate records.

## Website visits

This page does not include advertising trackers or an enquiry form. Hosting providers may process technical request information needed to deliver and protect the website. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the network requests needed to serve them.

## Enquiries

If you contact us by email or phone, provide only the information needed for your business enquiry. Do not send:

- Social Security numbers
- Inmate records
- Credentials
- Sensitive facility information

Any AI telephone assistant is a separate service and identifies itself at the start of a call.

## Customer deployments

Data handling, access, retention, and maintenance arrangements for a customer deployment will be agreed separately before installation. This public website is not a statement of customer-system certification.
