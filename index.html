04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Who is ReliefLedger for?

ReliefLedger focuses on correctional facilities where lieutenants manage overtime buckets, contact officers, and document assignment decisions. It brings those decisions into one place while keeping the lieutenant in control.

Can it follow our overtime policy?

Your facility’s rules guide the configuration. We work with your authorized policy owner to confirm eligibility, volunteer priority, seniority, tie-breakers, bucket updates, and exceptions before installation.

Does it replace our scheduling or payroll system?

No. ReliefLedger focuses on the overtime bucket and its decision record. Payroll, full workforce scheduling, and automated phone or radio contact are outside the standard package.

Will our information stay on site?

The deployment approach is customer-hosted, using infrastructure approved by your IT team. Hosting, access, backup, and maintenance responsibilities are agreed before installation. No inmate records are needed to manage overtime.

How do we get started?

Start with a short conversation about your facility’s overtime management needs. A blank form or fictional example is enough for discovery. We then identify the policy, IT, and purchasing contacts needed to agree on scope and next steps.
