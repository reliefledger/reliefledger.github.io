04 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

Is ReliefLedger available today?

ReliefLedger is in early product development. We’re speaking with correctional facilities to validate the workflow and agree a focused first implementation. The interface on this page is illustrative, not a deployed customer system.

Can it follow our overtime policy?

That is the design goal. We first need your authorized policy owner to confirm eligibility, volunteer priority, seniority, tie-breakers, bucket updates, and exceptions. We do not assume every facility follows the same rules.

Does it replace our scheduling or payroll system?

No. The first release focuses on the overtime call list and its decision record. Payroll, full workforce scheduling, and automated phone or radio contact are outside the initial scope.

Will our information stay on site?

Our proposed deployment is customer-hosted, using infrastructure approved by your IT team. Hosting, access, backup, and maintenance responsibilities are agreed before installation. No inmate records are needed for the proposed overtime workflow.

How do we get started?

Start with a short conversation about your current process. A blank form or fictional example is enough for discovery. We then identify the policy, IT, and purchasing contacts needed to assess a potential implementation.
