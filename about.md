# About ReliefLedger

ReliefLedger is a product of Sire Run, Inc., a company in San Francisco founded by David Ndungu. We build focused software for correctional facilities.

## What we build

ReliefLedger does one job: helping a lieutenant fill an unfilled post from the overtime bucket. The lieutenant sees who is next and why, under the facility's approved rules, records each contact outcome, and confirms the officer. Every assignment keeps a record that can be reviewed or printed.

It is not a payroll, timekeeping or full scheduling system, and it does not call officers or assign anyone on its own.

## Where we are

ReliefLedger is a new product. The demonstration on this website uses fictional officers and example rules. Early facilities shape the configuration around their own approved overtime policy.

## How we work with facilities

- We start with a 30-minute discovery call about how your facility fills an unfilled post today.
- Your authorized policy owner confirms the rules before anything is installed.
- ReliefLedger runs on infrastructure your IT team approves. No inmate records are needed.
- Each facility buys a one-time licence with remote setup, with optional annual maintenance.
