## Who it's for

ReliefLedger is for correctional facilities that fill unfilled posts from an overtime bucket: county and city jails, typically holding 250 to 2,000 people, and state prisons, where the corrections department sets overtime policy.

- **Sheriffs and jail administrators** own the jail's budget and overtime rules. ReliefLedger is a focused tool for one facility, bought with a one-time licence rather than a statewide system project.
- **Wardens and deputy wardens** sponsor a better process at their prison and bring in the department's policy, purchasing and IT contacts when there is a fit.
- **Majors, captains and lieutenants** work the bucket every shift. They see who is next and why, record each call, and confirm the officer, with the record kept for review.

## A good fit when

- The overtime bucket is kept on paper, a whiteboard or a spreadsheet.
- Call order depends on volunteers, seniority and tie-breaks applied by hand.
- Lieutenants need to show who was called, in what order, and why.
- Unfilled posts, holdovers or mandatory overtime come up often.
- You want a focused tool, not a full scheduling-system replacement.

Not a fit if call order already runs well inside a workforce scheduling system you are happy with, or if you need payroll, timekeeping or automated phone calls.

## The approach

1. **Know who's available.** Enter or import the roster, record which officers are present, and identify the posts and time blocks that need coverage.
2. **Review the bucket.** See officers in an order based on your approved policy, with clear reasons for eligibility and contact priority.
3. **Keep the decision.** Record contact outcomes, confirm assignments, and retain a printable history of what happened and why.

Facility rules, deployment requirements, and acceptance criteria are agreed with your team before installation.

**Who is ReliefLedger for?** Correctional facilities where lieutenants manage overtime buckets, contact officers, and document assignment decisions.
